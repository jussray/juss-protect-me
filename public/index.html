01 / PRIVACY

Less sensitive data.

Evidence contracts reject raw passwords, tokens, and other sensitive values. Provider identifiers are designed to remain pseudonymous.

02 / EVIDENCE

Proof with identity.

Receipts can be bound to model, policy, and sensor identities. Changed identities make previous proof stale.

03 / AUTHORITY

You remain in charge.

Recommendations do not automatically authorize consequential actions. Security and permission are separate decisions.
