Privacy

Plain words, because privacy is the product.

What we collect

  • Account email — the address you enroll with. It identifies your membership and nothing else.
  • Payment — handled entirely by Stripe. Your card number never reaches our servers.
  • Gateway checks (Model Two) — when your agent checks an action, we log the time, the tool name, and the allow/block decision with its reason. The content of the action is screened in the moment and is not stored.
  • Checkup answers (Model One) — stored only in your own browser, on your device. They are never sent to us.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your information. We do not run advertising trackers on these pages.
  • We do not collect sign-ups or payments on the marketing pages — enrollment happens only on the secure checkout page.

Your control

Cancel your membership and your entitlement switches off. Ask us through the contact page to delete your account record and we will.
