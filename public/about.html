About Juss Protect Me: people-first online safety

Juss Protect Me (Model One) is Juss & Co.'s personal cybersecurity project. Its purpose is to help people understand everyday online risks and take practical steps to protect accounts, devices, and personal information.

Who is Juss Protect Me for?

Individuals and families looking for approachable guidance about scam awareness, phishing, account security, device safety, and privacy. The focus is useful information without confusing security jargon.

How is Model One designed to help?

Model One is being developed around a guided safety checkup that explains common digital risks and suggests protective steps. Its public checkup is an informational preview, not a live device scan, antivirus service, continuous identity-monitoring service, or emergency response tool.

Is Juss Protect Me available now?

The product is in development. You can explore its current checkup preview and read about its goals, but early-access enrollment has not opened. Features described as planned are not promises of active protection.

How does this differ from Juss Protect Me 2?

Model One helps people understand personal digital safety. Juss Protect Me 2 (Model Two) focuses instead on AI-agent actions, permissions, and connected tools. Each product has its own scope.

Who is behind the product?

Juss Protect Me is developed by Juss & Co., founded by Raylene “Juss Ray” in Johnstown, Pennsylvania. Our approach is people-first, privacy-conscious, and clear about what has and has not been verified.
