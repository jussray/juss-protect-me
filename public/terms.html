Membership

Juss Protect Me products are memberships, billed through Stripe on the plan you choose at checkout. When payment completes, your account is created and your entitlement switches on automatically. If a payment fails, the entitlement pauses; when a subscription ends, it switches off.

What the products do — honestly

  • Model One is a guided safety checkup and education app. It does not scan your device, monitor your accounts, or block threats.
  • Model Two’s gateway checks agent actions against the boundaries you set and screens content for credential leaks using patterns. Pattern screening is a real boundary; it is not a guarantee that every harmful action or message will be caught.
  • This website’s own edge protection protects this website only.

Acceptable use

Use the gateway key only for your own agents and accounts. Do not share keys; rotate a key immediately if you believe it leaked. We may suspend keys used to attack the service or other people.

The products help you practice safer habits and enforce boundaries you set. They do not make any device, account, or agent invulnerable, and Juss & Co. is not liable for losses arising from actions taken outside the checks the products perform.
