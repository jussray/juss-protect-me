How Juss Protect Me Works

A personal cyber safety checkup you can understand and control.

Juss Protect Me helps people review everyday digital safety in plain language. Start with the available guided checkup, understand the steps, and choose what to improve. The current preview is not a live device scanner or threat-monitoring service.

Open the checkup previewSee the products

The process in three clear steps

01

Review your everyday safety

Open the guided checkup and review protections for your devices, accounts, passwords and personal information.

02

Make your choices

Mark protections you have already set up. The checkup explains practical steps for items you still want to address.

03

Keep your progress private

Your selections stay in this browser's local storage. Clearing browser data clears your saved checkup progress.

What is available, and what is not

Available to explore

The guided checkup preview is available to explore. It is a checklist with educational guidance, not an antivirus scan, identity-monitoring subscription, emergency response system, or a guarantee against cyberattacks.

Requires future setup or enrollment

Expanded protection and membership enrollment are not yet active. A website visit does not turn on device, identity or account monitoring.

Frequently asked questions

Does Juss Protect Me scan my phone or computer?

No. The available checkup preview asks you about security steps and provides guidance. It does not inspect your device or automatically block threats.

Where does my checkup information go?

The current checkup stores your selections locally in this browser. Clearing its local data removes the saved progress.

Is Juss Protect Me membership active yet?

Early-access enrollment has not opened. Any broader protection must be explicitly activated once it is available; visiting this page does not activate it.

How is Model One different from Model Two?

Model One focuses on people's everyday digital safety. Model Two focuses on authorization and oversight for connected AI agents and tools.
