Common questions about Juss Protect Me

Does the safety checkup scan my device?

No. The current preview is a guided checklist saved in your browser; it does not scan devices, monitor accounts, or block threats.

Can I buy active protection today?

No. Enrollment is not open. Each product shows its availability so you can distinguish the checkup preview from planned services.

How is this different from Juss Protect Me 2?

Model One focuses on personal and family digital safety. Model Two focuses on AI-agent, website, API, and software-release governance.

What happens next?

Membership enrollment is not open yet. Explore the available preview or review early access details. We will only describe live protection after its real-world activation is verified.
